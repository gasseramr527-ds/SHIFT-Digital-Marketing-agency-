# SHIFT-Digital-Marketing-agency-
Helping emerging consumer brands grow through strategic content and performance marketing.

SHIFT | Digital Marketing Agency

1. Project Name

SHIFT – Digital Marketing Agency

2. Project Idea

SHIFT is a digital marketing agency focused on helping emerging consumer brands grow through strategic marketing, content creation, social media management, paid advertising, and performance tracking.

The agency focuses on three main industries:

- Food & Beverage
- Fitness & Wellness
- Home & Lifestyle

Our goal is to help brands build a stronger digital presence through strategic and performance-driven marketing solutions.

4. Work Plan

The project is planned over three months:

- Phase 1: Market Research and Competitor Analysis.
- Phase 2: Brand Identity and Marketing Strategy.
- Phase 3: Content Strategy and Content Creation.
- Phase 4: Social Media Management and Promotion.
- Phase 5: Performance Tracking and Evaluation.

5. Roles & Responsibilities

All team members work collaboratively and share responsibilities throughout the project.

Our shared responsibilities include:

- Conducting market research and competitor analysis.
- Developing marketing strategies and growth plans.
- Creating content ideas and content calendars.
- Designing and producing marketing materials.
- Managing social media platforms.
- Planning and executing promotional campaigns.
- Monitoring performance and evaluating results.

All major decisions and tasks are discussed and carried out collaboratively by the team.

6. KPIs

-

The project's performance is measured through the following indicators:

Current Performance

- Content Production: 15 content pieces published.
- Audience Growth: 90 followers across social media platforms.

Performance Tracking

- Audience Reach: Measuring the number of unique accounts reached.
- Engagement Rate: Tracking likes, comments, shares, and saves.
- Profile Visits: Monitoring visits to social media profiles.
- Lead Generation: Tracking inquiries and potential clients.
